About the person behind the work

About Refat

Teacher. Builder. Quiet dreamer. I make apps, design learning materials, and try to make the classroom feel like a lighthouse - calm, bright, and purposeful. This personal space collects my projects, lessons, and a few ideas I keep scribbled in the margin.

My Story

My work usually starts with one question: will this actually help someone learn, understand, or do their work more calmly?

Foundation

Background

I teach mathematics and computer basics. I build useful small Android apps and web tools for students and teachers - simple, human-centered, and built with care.

Working Principles

What matters

Design for clarity first

I like interfaces that explain themselves quickly, reduce hesitation, and make people feel more capable instead of more confused.

Small steps, consistent practice, and generous curiosity. That mindset shows up in both teaching and software.
Where that leads

Practical outputs

  • Worksheets that can be reused without a lot of editing
  • Apps that feel approachable for students and teachers
  • Dashboards that support reflection instead of noise

What I Like Building
