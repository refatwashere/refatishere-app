How I Think About Projects

Product lens

Usefulness before decoration

I like work that earns its space. A project should have a clear reason to exist, a clear audience, and a shape that does not punish people for trying to use it.

Execution lens

Small systems that can grow

I would rather build a smaller system that feels coherent than a bigger one that becomes hard to explain. That is true for portfolio projects and for technical tools in this repo.
